# Findings: does a WhatsApp/Chatwoot conversation ever become a B2B SaaS lead?

**Change:** `chatwoot-b2b-lead-capture-investigation` (additional investigation subdomain of the
freemium-onboarding master plan, prompted by a founder question comparing Chatwoot's importance to
HubSpot's) · **Date:** 2026-08-28 · **Type:** investigation only, no code changed.

## Short answer

**No.** A prospect who fits Contexia's own routing rule for Campaña 2 (SaaS B2B) — a natural
person crossing UVT with continuous financial operations, or a persona jurídica — but who first
messages Taty on WhatsApp, is invisible to the system as a B2B lead. Nothing captures, flags, or
routes them. They stay inside the Renta Natural (Campaña 1) funnel indefinitely unless a human
operator happens to notice and act manually.

## Why

Three separate subsystems would each need to carry a "this is a B2B lead" signal for this to work,
and none of them do:

1. **Taty's WhatsApp classifier** only recognizes `sales_interest`, `payment_confirmation`, or
   `unknown` — all scoped to selling the Renta Natural tax-filing service. There is no
   "this sounds like a business" category.
2. **`crm_leads` (the table every WhatsApp lead lands in)** has no column for business type or
   lead-qualification tier, and nothing anywhere in the backend ever creates a `tenants`/
   `b2b_clients` row from a `crm_leads` row.
3. **Chatwoot's own attribute schema already has the fields you'd want** —
   `tipo_contribuyente: SAS` and `servicio_interes: creacion_empresa/CFO` are real dropdown
   options, provisioned back in August — but the automated pipeline that would populate them was
   explicitly deferred as a "fast-follow" when that schema shipped, and never built. Those values
   exist only for a human agent to type in manually while reading a conversation.

## Why this matters for the plan

Subdomain 2 found the opposite kind of surprise (persistence existed where the plan assumed a
gap). This is the mirror case: a gap exists where the plan never checked. Concretely, before
designing Subdomain 4 (`crm-alta-tiered-provisioning`), it's worth knowing that WhatsApp — despite
being Contexia's most active, live conversational channel — is not currently a source of B2B leads
at all, automated or otherwise, beyond a human operator's own judgment.

## Not decided here

This investigation deliberately does not recommend building the bridge, or say where it should
live (Taty's classifier? The Chatwoot tagging pipeline? Both?). That's a real scoping decision —
three separate subsystems, not a quick fix — that belongs to the founder to prioritize against the
rest of the freemium-onboarding roadmap, not something to bolt on silently.
